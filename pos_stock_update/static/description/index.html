POS Stock Update

Add stock to products from the POS interface with backend receipt creation

Step 1

Navigate to the Point of Sale module.

Step 2

Go to Dashboard and select the any one of the store. Then click on continue selling button.

Step 3

click the i icon in the product screen of POS.

Step 4

Here the Add Quantity Button visible as below in product information screen

Step 5

After clicking the Add Quantity button, a popup will open. Here, you can enter the required quantity in Nos .
If you have any notes, you can provide them in the Note field.

Step 6

After clicking on the Add button in the popup, a receipt will be created and the stock will be updated accordingly.
Any notes provided will be recorded in the Note tab of the receipt.

Step 7

After the successful creation of the receipt the sticky notes is visible within the pos screen as below.

Step 8

In previously this product has 15 available quantity after the add quantity of 5> now the available quantity is 20.
